Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 June 2026

Asteriq (“the app”) is developed by Mazzee Studio. This policy explains how the app handles your information. In short: it does not collect any.

Information we collect

Asteriq does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts, no servers operated by us, and no analytics or usage tracking of any kind.

Data stored on your device

All of your settings, mappings, and control profiles are stored locally on your own computer. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Network features

Asteriq uses the network only for two purposes:

  • Update checks. The app contacts GitHub to check whether a newer version is available. This sends no personal data; it only requests release information.
  • Input forwarding (optional). When you enable it, Asteriq sends controller input between computers that you configure. This data travels directly between your own devices and is never sent to us or any third party.

Third parties

Asteriq does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at github.com/MazzeeStudio/Asteriq/issues.
